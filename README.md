## Executive Abstract
Traditional mountain, forest, and off-grid trail lighting relies heavily on grid-tethered power cables, high-glare white LEDs that disrupt nocturnal ecosystems, and passive infrastructure incapable of sensing impending geological or hydrological hazards. **Off-Grid Bionic Smart Lighting 2.0** re-architects off-grid trail illumination into a self-sustaining **Forest Light-Water Symbiosis & Full-Sensory Disaster Early Warning Neural Network**.

Integrating biomimetic optical geometry, zero-grid micro-energy harvesting, four-dimensional vertical mountain hydrological sensing ("Living Mountain" profile), and colorimetric early-warning indicator strips, each autonomous lighting node acts simultaneously as an ecologically friendly low-glare pathfinder and a distributed environmental sentinel. Through ultra-low-power mesh telemetry, nodes continuously monitor soil saturation, slope micro-displacement, runoff turbidity, and micro-climate anomalies, providing real-time, localized visual and network alerts prior to landslides, debris flows, or flash floods—protecting both backcountry hikers and downstream communities without ecological intrusion.

## 核心架構升級亮點（v2.0.0 Core Upgrades）
1. **山林光水共生（Forest Light-Water Symbiosis）**：捨棄破壞林相的高架電纜與刺眼強光，採用仿生低眩光光學與獨立離網微儲能系統，與「活山」四維水文剖面自然耦合。
2. **全感官防災神經網（Full-Sensory Disaster Early Warning Neural Network）**：將照明節點升級為分散式防災哨兵，整合顯色試紙化學早警與邊坡微位移、土壤含水率及徑流濁度感測。
3. **斷網韌性與在地即時預警**：無須依賴外部市電與行動網路，透過超低功耗網狀通訊（Mesh）串聯步道節點，於山崩、土石流或溪水暴漲前夕提供視覺與遠端雙重早期預警。
